### Mobile support

The plugin now runs on iPhone, iPad and Android. It was wrongly flagged as desktop-only; nothing in it actually needs the desktop app. Feedback on layout or touch issues is welcome on GitHub. (#204)

### Long card titles no longer stretch every card

All cards on a board share the same height. A single very long note name used to make every card that tall. Card titles now stop at three lines with an ellipsis, and hovering a title shows it in full. (#207)

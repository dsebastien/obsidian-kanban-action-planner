### A real mobile experience, starting with the board

The board is now built for touch on phones and tablets.

- **Long-press to pick up a card**, then drag it to another column or position. Swiping over cards just scrolls, so you can no longer move a card by accident. Lift your finger without moving to open the card's menu (on iPhone and iPad, this was missing).
- **Auto-scroll while dragging**: hold a card near the edge of the board, a column or the lane stack and it scrolls that way. This works on the desktop too.
- **One column at a time on a phone**: each column fills the screen and the board snaps from column to column. A row of chips above the board shows every column with its card count; tap one to jump there.
- **Bigger touch targets**: column and lane buttons, the mode switch, and the resize handles in the ideal week and the timeline are sized for a finger, and the handles stay visible since there is no hover.
- **Printing the ideal week** isn't offered on mobile (the apps have no print dialog).

The calendar, timeline, WBS and ideal week still use their desktop gestures and layouts on a phone. Their mobile versions are planned next (#210 to #217). (#194)
